# Discussion Templates

GitHub Discussions do not support per-category markdown templates the way
issues do, but maintainers and frequent contributors can copy the starter text
below when opening a new thread in the relevant category. Keeping a consistent
shape makes Discussions easier to scan, easier to respond to, and easier to
graduate into issues or pull requests when an idea gains support.

This file lives at the repository root as `DISCUSSION_TEMPLATE.md` and the
house rules for the Discussions space are in
`.github/DISCUSSION_WELCOME_README.md`.

---

## Q&A — for questions about running, deploying, or extending BlockTrust

```markdown
**What I'm trying to do:**
<!-- A short description of your goal. -->

**What I've tried:**
<!-- Steps or approaches you've already attempted, and what happened. -->

**Environment:**
<!-- Browser + version, OS, whether you're running locally or on a deployed instance. -->

**What I'd like to know:**
<!-- Your specific question. -->
```

---

## Ideas — for feature proposals and product direction

```markdown
**The problem:**
<!-- What neighbourhood or user need does this address? -->

**The idea:**
<!-- A short description of the feature or change you're proposing. -->

**Where it fits:**
<!-- Which tab or part of the app, and which Roadmap phase if any. -->

**Possible approach:**
<!-- A rough sketch of how it could work. Optional but helpful. -->

**Willing to work on it:**
<!-- Yes / No / Need a buddy. -->
```

> If the idea is a long-term directional change, prefix the title with
> `Roadmap:` so maintainers can find it during roadmap reviews.

## Announcements — for project news

```markdown
**Title:**
<!-- A short headline. -->

**Summary:**
<!-- One or two sentences on the news. -->

**Details:**
<!-- The full announcement. -->

**What this means for contributors/users:**
<!-- Any action needed, e.g. "bump your cache", "update your fork". -->

**Links:**
<!-- Related PRs, issues, releases, or docs. -->
```

## Show and tell — for sharing your instance, fork, or customisation

```markdown
**What I built:**
<!-- A description of your instance, fork, or customisation. -->

**Why I built it:**
<!-- The neighbourhood or use case it serves. -->

**Screenshots:**
<!-- Before/after or just a nice shot of it running. -->

**How others can do the same:**
<!-- A link to your repo, a gist, or a short how-to. -->
```

## House rules (reminder)

The Discussions space follows the project's free-speech commitment: the only
content removed is hate speech, illegal content, and spam. Beyond that:

- Be civil. Attack ideas, not people. The Code of Conduct applies.
- Stay roughly on topic for the category you posted in.
- Search before posting — your question may already be answered.
- Use the templates above as a starting point so threads are easy to read.
